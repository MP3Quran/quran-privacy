Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This privacy policy is for the mobile apps published on Google Play by the developer Quran Audio Library:

  • Qur'an tiếng Việt
  • อัลกุรอานภาษาไทย (Thai Quran)

Quran Audio Library provides these apps so people can read the Quran and its meanings and listen to recitation. This page describes only these apps. It is not the privacy policy of the mp3quran.net website.

Information these apps collect

These apps do not ask you to create an account. They do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. They do not use the advertising ID. They do not include advertising or analytics software.

Information stored on your device

The apps may save reading position and display preferences on your device only. That information stays on the device and is not sent to Quran Audio Library.

Network use

When you open the text or play recitation, the app downloads that content from mp3quran.net. Your device’s network address is used only to deliver the request. Quran Audio Library does not use these apps to build a profile of you, and it does not sell personal information.

Sharing

If you use the share button, your device’s own share menu sends the text. Quran Audio Library does not receive your contacts or the list of people you share with.

Children

The apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

Privacy questions about these apps can be sent to Quran Audio Library at bnmnet@gmail.com, or through https://mp3quran.net/eng/contact-us.
